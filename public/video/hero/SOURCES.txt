Opening-page film: made for COZCAF with Google Flow (Veo 3.1 video, Nano Banana images) on the client's Google AI Pro plan,
then edited into one loop. Order: Coffee, camera move, Cold Drinks, move, Snacks & Sides, move, Mains, move back to Coffee.
Each dish shot is 8 s; each camera move is 5.33 s (an 8 s Veo clip at 1.5x). The COZCAF logos on the cup and bucket are the real lockup.
Every clip carries Google's small "Veo" mark (visible watermarking is required in the client's region).
  cozcaf-opening.mp4       720x1280, the film itself
  cozcaf-opening.webp      first frame, shown until the film plays
  cozcaf-opening-soft.mp4  a tiny copy, blurred behind the film on wide screens
Master files (1080x1920) are kept outside the repo.
