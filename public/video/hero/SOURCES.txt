Opening-page film: made for COZCAF with Google Flow (Veo 3.1 video, Nano Banana images) on the client's Google AI Pro plan,
then edited into one loop. Order (v3): Coffee, camera move, Cold Drinks (Ice Toffee, Rose Latte, Ice Mocha), move, Burgers & Wraps (Zinger Burger,
Zinger Wrap, Cheesy Chicken Loaded Fries), move, Pasta (Arrabbiata, Alfredo, Creamy Tomato), move back to Coffee.
Each dish shot is 8 s; each camera move is 5.33 s. The camera moves dip off one table into the smoke over the marble and rise onto
the next, joined while only smoke is in frame (no dish ever turns into another). The COZCAF logos on the cup, the bucket and the
the wrap box and the Alfredo bowl are the real lockup (printed onto the footage and tracked with the camera).
Every clip carries Google's small "Veo" mark (visible watermarking is required in the client's region).
  cozcaf-opening.mp4       720x1280, the film itself
  cozcaf-opening-1080.mp4  1080x1920, for large / high-density screens
  cozcaf-opening.webp      first frame, shown until the film plays
  smoke1.webp, smoke2.webp Nano Banana smoke stills, drifting behind the film on wide screens
Master files (1080x1920) are kept outside the repo.
