# The opening page

The opening page was redesigned after the first sign-off. The original cup / bean scene (a pixel-exact port of the approved prototype) is gone, and so is the comparison harness that checked it.

- **Opening film:** the opening page is one looping film of the cafe's own dishes (about 53 s): Coffee, Cold Drinks, Snacks & Sides, Mains, with a real camera move from table to table (no slides or cuts). It was made with Google Flow (Veo 3.1) from the client's dish pictures; the COZCAF lettering on the cup sleeve and bucket is the real logo. Phones show it full screen; wide screens show it as a tall panel right of centre, with a soft blurred copy filling the sides and the COZCAF heading, tagline and category in the left column. Files are in `public/video/hero` (`cozcaf-opening.mp4` 720x1280 about 6 MB, `-soft.mp4` for the blurred sides, `.webp` first frame). The first frame shows at once as an image; the film starts 0.6 s after the page has loaded and fades in over it. See `SOURCES.txt` (Veo's small visible watermark is kept, as Google's terms require).
- **Category chip:** "01 Coffee" etc. with four dots under the tagline, following the film (the word changes halfway through each camera move). Tap a dot to jump to that table; hovering or focusing it pauses the film. Reduced motion and Data Saver show only the first frame and download no video.
- **Kerala map (new section, before Visit):** like the reference site's network map, told honestly for one cafe: Kerala in moss with its 14 districts, COZCAF's pin at Parappanangadi (Malappuram) with a tag in the real logo lettering, and a slow ripple spreading from it across the state that lights Kozhikode, Kochi and Thiruvananthapuram as it passes. Beside it: "1 cafe and counting", "1 of 14 districts", "Since 2021", a "Next stop: your town?" card, Get directions and Follow the journey (Instagram). Outline from geohacker/kerala census districts (ODbL), simplified into `src/data/kerala-map.ts`. Still frame under reduced motion.
- **Playback:** the video starts about 1.8 s after load (the poster is the same frame, so the hand-over is seamless), pauses when the tab is hidden or the page has scrolled over it, and retries on the first touch if the browser refused autoplay.
- **Heading:** the COZCAF lettering is the header's logo. At the top of the page it is lifted into a big centred heading; on the first scroll it glides into the header and the bean mark fades in (`src/hero/page.ts`). There is no second logo.
- **Kept from the old design:** header with nav and Find us, tagline, rotating badge, open-now chip, the left and right copy on desktop, and the cream ticker.
- **Phones:** lighter render path (no grade/bloom/grain passes, about 30 frames a second, lower resolution) and no side copy.
- **Reduced motion:** one finished frame, and the heading jumps instead of gliding.
- **Not available:** the sound toggle, Skip and Replay of the old intro.
